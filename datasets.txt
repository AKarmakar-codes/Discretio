1. IDD-PeD (Indian Driving Dataset - Pedestrian) [ANCHOR DATASET]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Primary reference for unstructured traffic, dense interactions, non-signalized
  crossings, and Indian behavioral ground truth.
- Perspective: Egocentric front-facing vehicle camera (1080p GoPro at 10-30 Hz).
- Volume: ~5,000 pedestrians, ~205,000 annotated frames, 9 video sets.
- Key Annotations for Behavioral Prior:
  * Age Brackets: child, teenager, adult, senior (native in released annotations)
  * Gender: male, female, default
  * Crossing Behaviors: crossing (yes/no), crossing motive (yes/maybe/no), crossing point (frame id)
  * Crossing Dynamics: crossing unobstructed (CU), crossing diagonal (CD), crossing interrupted/hesitation (CI), jaywalking
  * Actions: walking, moving swiftly (sprinting), standing, sitting
  * Attention & Gaze: looking over shoulder (LOS), facing towards traffic (FTT), not looking (NL), distracted behavior (DB)
  * Vehicle Telemetry: Ego speed and GPS at 10 Hz
- Access Links & Repositories:
  * GitHub: https://github.com/Ruthvik9/IDD-PeD
  * Hugging Face: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Ruthvik9/IDD-PeD
  * Project Page: https://cvit.iiit.ac.in/research/projects/cvit-projects/iddped
  * Videos: Hosted in 9 tar files (gp_set_0001.tar to gp_set_0009.tar, ~9 GB each)
    URL pattern: https://cvit.iiit.ac.in/images/datasets/IDDPed/Videos/gp_set_0001.tar
- Practical Note: Download video tarballs one at a time to manage local disk space.


2. JAAD (Joint Attention for Autonomous Driving) [CORE DATASET]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Primary structured Western reference for pedestrian-driver communication,
  demographic priors, and crosswalk behavior.
- Perspective: Egocentric dashcam (1080p at 30 fps).
- Volume: 346 video clips (5-10 sec each), ~82,000 frames, ~2,793 pedestrian tracks.
- Key Annotations for Behavioral Prior:
  * Age Brackets: child, young-adult, adult, senior
  * Gender: male, female
  * Behavioral Actions: standing, walking, crossing vs not-crossing, hesitation
  * Attention / Gaze: looking at vehicle vs not-looking, nodding, gesturing
  * Scene Context: designated crosswalk (yes/no), traffic signal (red/green/none)
- Access Links & Repositories:
  * GitHub: https://github.com/ykotseruba/JAAD (Branch: JAAD_2.0)
  * Full Video Archive (3.08 GB): https://data.nvision2.eecs.yorku.ca/JAAD_dataset/data/JAAD_clips.zip


3. PIE (Pedestrian Intent Estimation) [CORE DATASET]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Unbiased continuous base rate reference for crossing intention and high-precision
  kinematic ground truth in structured Western traffic.
- Perspective: Egocentric front-facing vehicle camera (1080p at 30 fps) + OBD-II sensors.
- Volume: 6 hours of continuous driving in downtown Toronto, 53 sequences, ~293,000 frames,
  1,842 pedestrians.
- Key Annotations for Behavioral Prior:
  * Age Brackets: child, adult, senior
  * Crossing Intention: binary crossing intent (0/1) with critical crossing event timestamps
  * Behavioral Actions: walking, standing, looking/attention
  * Vehicle Dynamics: calibrated 30 Hz OBD-II speed, longitudinal/lateral acceleration, GPS, compass heading
- Access Links & Repositories:
  * GitHub: https://github.com/aras62/PIE
  * Utilities & Dataloaders: Included directly in the repository


4. TITAN (Trajectory Inference and Tracking in Autonomous Navigation) [CORE DATASET]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Structured Asian mega-city reference with dense multi-agent coordination,
  layered action taxonomy, and communicative cues.
- Perspective: Egocentric front-facing vehicle camera (1080p GoPro at 60 fps) + IMU/GPS.
- Volume: 700 video clips (10-20 sec each), Tokyo downtown streets.
- Key Annotations for Behavioral Prior:
  * Age Brackets: child, adult, senior
  * Hierarchical Actions: 50 distinct action labels (crossing road, waiting to cross,
    walking along road, looking at phone, pushing cart/pram)
  * Communicative Actions: looking, nodding, hand gesturing
  * Vehicle Telemetry: Synchronized IMU acceleration and angular velocity
- Access Links & Repositories:
  * Official Portal: https://usa.honda-ri.com/titan
  * Access: Academic request form via institutional university email
